# Frequently asked questions — Demo Physio Clinic (fictional)

> DEMO DATA ONLY. This is a synthetic concept demo. There is no real clinic, no real patient data and no real client behind these documents.

## Booking an appointment

Book by phone or online. Tell us the reason for your visit, so we can allow the right amount of time. If you are a new patient, arrive 10 minutes early to complete a health form.

## Do I need a GP referral?

No. You do not need a GP referral to book a physiotherapy appointment. You can book directly. A referral is useful if you have one, and it is needed for some funding claims, such as an EPC plan or a WorkCover claim — see `bulk-billing.md`.

## Telehealth appointments

Yes, we offer telehealth physiotherapy appointments by video. A telehealth appointment is a video consultation for advice, exercise review and progress checks. It is not the same as an in-person appointment: the physiotherapist cannot do hands-on treatment over video. You need a device with a camera and a quiet space where you can move.

## Cancelling or changing an appointment

If you need to cancel or change your appointment, please contact the clinic at least 24 hours before your appointment time. If you cancel with less than 24 hours notice, or if you do not attend, a cancellation fee of $40 may apply. The cancellation fee is charged because the appointment time cannot be offered to another patient at short notice.

## Parking and getting here

Free on-street parking is available on the streets around the clinic, with a 2-hour limit. There is a disabled parking space at the front of the building. The clinic is on the ground floor, with step-free access from the street.

## What should I wear?

Wear comfortable clothes you can move in. For a knee or hip problem, wear or bring shorts. For a shoulder problem, wear a singlet or a loose top. You will be asked to move during the assessment, so avoid tight clothing.

## Can I bring someone with me?

Yes. You can bring a family member, carer or support person to your appointment.
