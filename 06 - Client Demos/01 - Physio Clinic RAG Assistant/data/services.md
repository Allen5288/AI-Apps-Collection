# Services — Demo Physio Clinic (fictional)

> DEMO DATA ONLY. This is a synthetic concept demo. There is no real clinic, no real patient data and no real client behind these documents.

## Physiotherapy services

- **Initial physiotherapy consultation** — a 45-minute assessment for a new injury or condition. The physiotherapist takes a history, examines the area and agrees a treatment plan with you.
- **Follow-up physiotherapy consultation** — a 30-minute session for an existing injury or condition, including hands-on treatment and exercise progression.
- **Sports physiotherapy** — assessment and treatment of sports injuries, including sprains, strains, tendon pain and return-to-sport planning.
- **Back and neck pain** — assessment and treatment of lower back pain, neck pain and stiffness, including exercise-based rehabilitation.
- **Post-operative rehabilitation** — guided rehabilitation after surgery, such as knee or shoulder surgery, following your surgeon's instructions.
- **Telehealth physiotherapy** — a video consultation for advice, exercise review and progress checks when an in-person visit is not needed. See `faqs.md` for how telehealth works.

## Your first visit — what to bring

Bring a referral letter if you have one, a list of your current medicines, any recent scans or reports, and comfortable clothes you can move in. Wear or bring shorts for a knee or hip problem, and a singlet or loose top for a shoulder problem.

You do not need a GP referral to book a physiotherapy appointment. See `faqs.md` for booking and referral questions.

## What we do not do

We do not diagnose from a message or a photo, we do not order scans, and we do not give medical advice about medicines. If you have chest pain, severe weakness, or a new problem with bladder or bowel control, seek urgent medical care.
