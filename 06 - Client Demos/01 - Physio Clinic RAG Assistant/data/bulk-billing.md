# Bulk billing, Medicare and health funds — Demo Physio Clinic (fictional)

> DEMO DATA ONLY. This is a synthetic concept demo. Funding and billing details are invented for the demo. There is no real clinic, no real patient data and no real client behind these documents.

## Bulk billing

We do not bulk bill physiotherapy appointments. There is no bulk-billed physiotherapy appointment at this clinic. A fee applies to every appointment, as listed in `prices-and-hours.md`.

## Medicare and Enhanced Primary Care (EPC) plans

If you have a Medicare Enhanced Primary Care (EPC) plan, also called a Chronic Disease Management plan, from your GP, you may be able to claim a Medicare rebate for up to five allied health sessions per calendar year. Bring your EPC plan and your Medicare card to your appointment. We do not bulk bill EPC sessions, so you pay the appointment fee and then claim the Medicare rebate yourself. The rebate amount is set by Medicare, not by this clinic.

## Private health insurance

If you have private health insurance with extras cover that includes physiotherapy, you may be able to claim part of the appointment fee from your health fund. Bring your health fund card or your health fund membership details. We can provide an itemised receipt for your claim. The amount your fund pays depends on your level of cover, so check with your health fund before your appointment.

## NDIS and WorkCover

We accept National Disability Insurance Scheme (NDIS) participants and WorkCover claims where the claim has been approved and the referral paperwork is in place. Bring your NDIS plan details or your WorkCover claim number and insurer details. Talk to us before you book if you are unsure whether your claim covers physiotherapy.

## What to bring for a claim

Bring your Medicare card, your EPC plan if you have one, and your private health fund card if you want to claim. Without these details we cannot help you with a rebate or a claim.
