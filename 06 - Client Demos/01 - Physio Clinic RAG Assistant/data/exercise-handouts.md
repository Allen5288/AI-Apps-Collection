## How handouts work

Your physiotherapist gives you a home exercise handout at your appointment, matched to your condition. Each handout shows the exercise, how many repetitions to do, and what to do if it hurts. Do the exercises as shown on your own handout, not from a general list.

## Lower back exercise handout

- **Pelvic tilt (lying):** Lie on your back with your knees bent. Gently flatten your lower back into the floor, hold for 5 seconds, then relax. Repeat 10 times, twice a day.
- **Knee-to-chest stretch:** Lie on your back, bring one knee towards your chest and hold for 20 seconds. Repeat 3 times on each side.
- **Bridge:** Lie on your back with knees bent, lift your hips until your body is in a straight line from shoulders to knees, hold for 5 seconds, then lower slowly. Repeat 10 times.
- Stop and contact the clinic if an exercise causes sharp pain, numbness, or pain that lasts more than an hour after the exercise.

## Knee exercise handout

- **Straight-leg raise:** Sit or lie down, tighten the muscle on the front of your thigh, then lift your straight leg a short distance off the bed. Hold for 5 seconds, then lower slowly. Repeat 10 times.
- **Mini squat:** Stand holding a chair for balance, bend your knees a small amount, then straighten up. Repeat 10 times. Do not let knee pain go above a mild ache.

## Shoulder exercise handout

- **Pendulum exercise:** Lean forward, let your sore arm hang, and gently swing it in small circles. Do this for 1 minute, twice a day.
- **Wall walk:** Stand facing a wall, walk your fingers up the wall as far as comfortable, hold for 10 seconds, then walk them back down. Repeat 5 times.

## When to ask for help

Contact the clinic if you are unsure how to do an exercise, if your pain is getting worse, or if you have lost the handout. Do not start a new exercise from this document without first talking to your physiotherapist.
